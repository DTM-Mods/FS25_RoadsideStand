<p align="center">
  <b>A roadside selling stand you can place on your own farm.</b>
</p>

<p align="center">
  <a href="https://github.com/DTM-Mods/FS25_RoadsideStand/releases/latest"><img src="https://img.shields.io/badge/Download-latest%20release-D9A441?style=for-the-badge&logo=github&logoColor=143326&labelColor=E9C77A" alt="Download the latest release"></a>
</p>

<p align="center">
  <img src="https://raw.githubusercontent.com/DTM-Mods/.github/main/assets/features.jpg" alt="Features" width="100%">
</p>

- **Accepts** field crops and windrows (grass, hay, straw, alfalfa, clover, flax, hemp, legume straw; including ones added by your map and other mods), bales, wood chips and rope pallets.
- **Doesn't accept** anything else: no milk, other products, fuel or inputs.
- Tip crops, windrows and wood chips into the pit, and drop bales and rope pallets in the bale zone. Both are on the same lane.
- Pays 100% of the base price. Flooding it with one crop lowers the price, which recovers over 48 in-game hours.
- Price: 100,000.

<p align="center">
  <img src="https://raw.githubusercontent.com/DTM-Mods/.github/main/assets/installation.jpg" alt="Installation" width="100%">
</p>

Put `FS25_RoadsideStand.zip` in `Documents/My Games/FarmingSimulator2025/mods`. In multiplayer, install it on the server and on every player's game.
